Standing still, L1 or R1 makes Croc hop sideways. Unlike his other moves
his speed stays zero: the step comes from the sidestep animation's root
motion (the object loop 0x8002C790 applies each frame's displacement), so
it needs Croc's animations imported from the disc. Not in the goal
yet: the person decided it joins the goal once psx's plan step 22 has
brought in the game's assets (animations among them). It was
found while covering F-0001's branches. To record it: from start_1_1, L1
for 10 ticks, then R1 for 10 ticks (Croc moves about 260 units each way).
